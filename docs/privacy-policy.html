Summary

Finances is a personal budgeting app. Your financial records are stored only on your device. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. It connects to the internet only to fetch exchange rates and, if you choose to set it up with your own API key, stock market data.

Information stored on your device

The following is stored in the app's private storage on your phone and is not sent to us or anyone else:

  • Transactions, accounts, categories, keyword rules, budgets and recurring payment information.
  • Receipt records: merchant, date, amounts and payment method that you confirm. The photo and the recognized text are not kept.
  • Notification processing results: the app that posted the notification, the time and the outcome (for example "promotion ignored"). These are deleted after 30 days. Notification text is not stored.
  • Exchange rates, your stock watchlist and the last quote for each watched symbol.
  • Settings, including a verifier derived from your PIN (never the PIN itself) and, if you add one, your market data API key encrypted with an Android Keystore key.

Backup

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app's data. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, your data is permanently deleted.

Notification access

This feature is optional and is only enabled after you read an in-app disclosure and choose "Continue", and then grant Notification Access in Android settings. When enabled, the app reads the title and text of notifications from these apps only: Revolut, Google Wallet, OTP Bank, Wise, Erste Bank (George) and MBH Bank. It uses them on your device to recognize the amount, currency, merchant, card ending and time of payments. Notifications from all other apps are ignored. Promotional notifications and security notifications, such as login codes, are rejected. Notification contents are not stored and are not sent off your device. You can stop this in the app at any time and revoke Notification Access in Android settings.

Camera and receipts

The camera is used only while you are on the receipt scanning screen. Text recognition runs on your device using Google ML Kit. Images are not saved or uploaded by the app. Google ML Kit is provided under Google's terms, which may include sending Google limited diagnostic information about the use of the ML Kit library; see Google's ML Kit documentation for details.

Stock market data

The stock watchlist displays market information from Finnhub. Quotes may be delayed and are labelled with the time they were last updated. The feature is for information only, does not offer trading and is not investment advice. Watched stocks are kept separate from your financial records and do not affect balances.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not collect your financial data and we do not sell or share it.

Data retention and deletion

  • Your records stay on your device until you delete them.
  • Settings > Data management > Delete all financial data removes transactions, accounts, categories, rules, budgets, receipts and notification results, while keeping your app lock and settings.
  • Settings > Data management > Reset app completely removes everything, including your PIN, API key and watchlist.
  • Notification processing results are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Security

You can protect the app with a PIN and optional biometric unlock. The PIN is never stored; a salted, slow-hashed verifier bound to an Android Keystore key is stored instead. The Finnhub API key is encrypted with an Android Keystore key. All network requests use HTTPS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your phone itself protected with a screen lock.

Permissions

  • Internet: exchange rates and optional market data.
  • Camera: receipt scanning, requested only when you open the scanner.
  • Notification access: optional, granted by you in Android settings.
  • Biometric: optional fingerprint or face unlock.
