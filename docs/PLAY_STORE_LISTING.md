**App name:** Finances

**Short description (80 characters max):**
Budget tracker that keeps your money records on your phone.

**Full description:**

Finances is a personal budgeting app that keeps your transactions on your device. There is no account to create and no cloud sync.

Add expenses and income by hand, scan the total from a paper receipt with on-device text recognition, or import a bank statement in CSV format. If you use Revolut or Google Wallet, Finances can also recognize card payments from their notifications once you allow notification access. Payments from a few Hungarian banks and Wise are recognized as well and placed in a review queue for you to confirm. Promotional and security notifications are ignored, and notification text is not saved.

Track accounts in several currencies, see monthly income and spending by category, set monthly budgets and spot recurring payments. Amounts in other currencies are converted with European Central Bank reference rates or rates you enter yourself.

A simple stock watchlist shows quotes and price history from Finnhub using your own Finnhub API key. Each quote shows when it was last updated, and delayed prices are labelled as delayed. The watchlist is information only: it cannot trade and it is not investment advice.

Protect the app with a PIN and optional fingerprint unlock. Delete your financial data at any time from the settings.

Finances is not a bank and does not connect to your bank accounts.
